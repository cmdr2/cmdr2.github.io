After over a year of frenetic fun coding using agents, I finally miss the calm and deep immersion of working on a complex task on my own.

This is not to say that I don't want to use agents anymore, or that I want to return to the earlier way of working. I'm just surprised at feeling this sense of nostalgia, since I've had zero issues so far transitioning to working through agents and still feeling the [sense of "agency"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sense_of_agency).

But it sometimes feels like someone else doing the puzzles for you :)
